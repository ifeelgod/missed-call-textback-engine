## 1. The "6:30 PM Missed Call Audit" SOP & Pitch Engine

### 1.1 Step-by-Step Outbound Audit Protocol

1. **Time Window:** 6:30 PM – 7:30 PM Local Time (After typical office business hours).
2. **Search Query:** Open Google Maps and search for local trade categories:
   - `Plumbers in [CITY]`
   - `HVAC Contractors in [CITY]`
   - `Roofing Repair in [CITY]`
   - `Electricians in [CITY]`
3. **Execution:** Call 10 local listings directly from your phone.
4. **Diagnostic Criteria:**
   - If answered live: Politely say "Sorry, wrong number" and hang up.
   - If rings >4 times, drops to voicemail, and **NO automated text-back arrives within 3 minutes**: Mark as **HIGH-INTENT PROSPECT**.
5. **Screen Recording Asset Creation:**
   - Open screen recorder (Loom or phone screen record).
   - Show Google Maps listing of the contractor.
   - Show your phone call log with timestamp (e.g., 6:34 PM).
   - Show empty SMS inbox proving no auto-reply was received.
