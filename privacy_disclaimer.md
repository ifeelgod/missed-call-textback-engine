## 5. Telephony Data Protection & TCPA Compliance Disclosures

### 5.1 Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) Compliance
All text messages dispatched by our system adhere to TCPA guidelines:
- Every auto-reply message includes mandatory opt-out instructions: `"Reply STOP to cancel"`.
- When a customer replies `STOP`, `UNSUBSCRIBE`, or `CANCEL`, their phone number is committed to an immediate suppression database, preventing any future text automated messages.

### 5.2 A2P 10DLC Brand Transparency
Carrier registration information (EIN, Business Address) is transmitted securely to The Campaign Registry (TCR) solely for brand verification and spam prevention.
