## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

#### Q: Do I need to know how to code to deploy this?
**A:** No! The entire workflow is pre-built in **N8N** (a visual workflow automation tool). You simply download our included JSON schema file, click "Import" in N8N, and paste in your Twilio or Telnyx API keys.

#### Q: How much does N8N and Twilio cost me to run?
**A:** N8N can be self-hosted on a \$5/month Cloud VPS (or run on N8N Cloud). Twilio messaging costs roughly \$0.0083 per SMS. For a typical contractor receiving 30 missed calls a month, your total carrier cost is under \$3.50/month per client. You pocket the remaining **\$145.50/month** as net recurring profit.

#### Q: Will local contractors actually pay $297 setup + $149/month for this?
**A:** Yes! A single saved plumbing or HVAC repair job pays contractor clients between \$500 and \$2,000. Saving just ONE missed call per month yields a 300%+ ROI for their business.

#### Q: What if a contractor doesn't want to change their phone number?
**A:** They don't have to! Our specification uses **Conditional Call Forwarding (CCF)**. The contractor keeps their existing Google Voice, Verizon, or AT&T business phone number. The phone only forwards to your system when they fail to answer after 4 rings.

#### Q: How do I handle 10DLC carrier compliance?
**A:** Module 6 of the spec provides a step-by-step registration guide for Twilio/Telnyx A2P 10DLC brand approval, ensuring 100% deliverability and preventing carrier spam filtering.
